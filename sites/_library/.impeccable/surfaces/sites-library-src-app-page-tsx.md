Scope: the composed one-page site for an Irish trade or local business (every preset, every variant). Mode: Persuade.

Audience / job: a local person on a phone choosing who to ring; the owner judging their own demo. Action: call, WhatsApp, or quote request. Proof: real name, town, coverage, Google rating and real review text. Constraints: thin data, frequently no photos/email/reviews; every word editable via content.json.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The van and the map: the business's name, trade, phone and the towns it covers are the design. Refuses the category default of a stock-photo hero with a centred slogan and an icon-card services grid.

OWN-WORLD: Four presets sharing one grammar: phone number set as display type, a pin-led coverage line of real town names, one brand accent recomputed for contrast, tone-alternating sections, no chrome where content belongs. Heritage = painted shopfront fascia; industrial = site hoarding and spec sheet; clean-local = friendly mist-and-white; bold = van livery at poster scale.

STORY: Within one screen the visitor knows who, what trade, which towns, and how to call; scrolling adds services, how it works, real reviews and the owner's voice; the page ends on a contact block and the call bar never leaves.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Business name via BrandMark top-left, phone top-right on desktop; hero headline naming trade + town at display scale; one-line subhead; coverage line with pin; primary Call button and secondary quote button; image only when a real/approved photo exists; sticky Call/WhatsApp bar pinned at the bottom on phones.

FORM: Brief-pinned four-preset system (the brief wins); the degraded roll's assigned index 6 (the local map / Ordnance Survey grammar) is carried as the shared coverage motif. Seed key af980029.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
