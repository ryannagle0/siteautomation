# Inspiration notes

Seven Dribbble references from `inspiration/`. The filenames are hashes, so each is labelled R1–R7 here. The videos were reviewed as 9-frame contact sheets across their full length.

**Rule for everything below:** only patterns and principles are taken. Nothing is recreated shot-for-shot, and no illustrations, photos, icons, logos or text are reused. Every idea is rebuilt from our tokens, `content.json` and the lead's own assets. Each idea has to serve the one job these pages have: a local customer on a phone deciding to ring.

---

## R1: art-marketplace landing page (video)
`4d32e42469657663b66a3c08aeccd70e.mp4`

- **For:** a whole-page direction, with notable hero, bento and "ticker band" moments.
- **Layout:**
  - A centred headline over a fanned, overlapping stack of artwork cards.
  - Later: a cloud of circular avatars around a centred line, and a 2×2 bento with mixed tile sizes (one tall image tile beside text tiles).
  - Also two large image tiles with the text set inside them.
  - A full-width lime marquee band of words with small inline images.
- **Typography:** a neutral grotesk throughout. Headlines are medium weight at big sizes with tight tracking; body text is tiny. The contrast comes from scale, not weight.
- **Colour roles:** a near-white ground and pale grey surfaces. All the colour comes from the content (the artworks). One acid-lime accent is used once, for the marquee.
- **Spacing:** very airy, with each section filling a viewport. Density is low apart from the bento.
- **Premium details:**
  1. The marquee band: moving type with small inline visuals, used once as a page-level punctuation mark.
  2. The bento mixes one big visual tile with small text tiles; the big tile gets the image, the small ones the words.
  3. Content (the images) carries the colour, and the chrome stays neutral.
- **Fits:** bold (the marquee) and clean-local (the airy neutral chrome). It doesn't suggest a new preset.
- **Not for us:** the avatar clouds, pricing cards, logo walls and "trusted by" rows. They'd be fake or irrelevant for a sole trader.

## R2: "Longbow" car launch (still)
`5a5f02ddab418a746aff3dd7a78f4d24.webp`

- **For:** the hero.
- **Layout:**
  - A full-bleed studio photo divided by **visible hairline grid lines** into a 4×2 grid of cells.
  - The product name is set enormous, condensed, in white, anchored bottom-left and spanning two columns.
  - A secondary text block sits inside one grid cell, bottom-right.
  - The nav is a small centred pill.
- **Typography:** a heavy condensed display for the name (huge scale contrast), a small serif italic tagline, and a monospace caps paragraph. That's three voices, each with one job.
- **Colour roles:** monochrome grey photo and white type. The only colour is the product itself (the tan seats, the red tail lights).
- **Spacing:** the grid does the spacing, with every element snapped to a cell.
- **Premium details:**
  1. Hairline grid lines drawn over the photo, so the layout's structure becomes the ornament.
  2. The wordmark sits on the bottom edge like a signature on a poster.
  3. The copy block sits inside a cell rather than floating.
- **Fits:** industrial (a condensed display, and gridded like a spec sheet). It also works for heritage with its serif.
- **Not for us:** the mono paragraph, which is costume for "technical" and breaks our type rules; and the tiny centred pill nav. The phone must stay visible.

## R3: AI "creative operations" SaaS landing page (still)
`ae44e21b9c282e9189939c9d8af5404f.webp`

- **For:** the hero.
- **Layout:** a centred headline, a one-line subhead, then **a single large input box as the focal point** with chips inside it (options), a logo row, then a split section with stats.
- **Typography:** a grotesk headline with an italic second line, and a medium weight.
- **Colour roles:** a blue glow gradient on navy, fading to white. Heavy "tech" colour.
- **Spacing:** a centred single column.
- **Premium details:**
  1. The page's main action is an **input you can type into straight away**, not a button.
  2. Chips inside the box make the next choice one tap.
- **Fits:** none as a look. Its SaaS look, blue/purple glow gradient, logo wall and stat counters are all on our anti-reference list.
- **Kept:** only the interaction idea. A hero whose focal point is a one-line "What needs doing?" box with service chips and a send button, beside the phone number. For a tradesperson that turns the hero into the quote request itself.

## R4: "Tech Recruit" colourways (video)
`cc1a5ee78c4fa86b5196f2b956ebcdc0.mp4`

- **For:** the hero, plus a mega-menu nav.
- **Layout:**
  - The hero is one **saturated colour field** (blue, orange or green versions) with a soft tonal wave.
  - The headline sits mid-left with a small "+" crosshair beside it.
  - **Along a hairline near the bottom: a row of 3–4 sector labels in columns**, then the CTA pair bottom-right, then a logo row.
  - The nav opens into a white mega-menu of three ruled columns.
- **Typography:** a light-to-regular grotesk headline, not bold; confident through size and space. The labels are tiny caps.
- **Colour roles:** one colour owns the whole hero ("committed/drenched"), and the white UI chrome sits on it. The same layout ships in several colours, which is exactly our "brand accent at build time" model.
- **Spacing:** a generous hero, with structure given by hairlines and "+" corner marks.
- **Premium details:**
  1. A drenched brand-colour field with a subtle tonal gradient of **one** hue: depth without a second colour.
  2. The service categories are set as a ruled row inside the hero, so you see what they do in the first screen.
  3. "+" registration marks at the grid corners: a quiet, precise detail.
- **Fits:** bold and clean-local (the drenched accent), and industrial on a dark ground. The colour-swap system is our model exactly.
- **Not for us:** the client logo row (a fake claim for a local trade).

## R6: "Tech Recruit" inner pages (video)
`d59b392da269eee78d76aa65c871ed01.mp4`

- **For:** services, about, stats, testimonials, case study, awards and contact.
- **Layout:**
  - Services as a **4×2 table of cells sharing hairline borders**: a line icon top-left and the label bottom-left, with the whole cell tappable.
  - An "approach" split with a black-and-white photo strip.
  - A drenched blue stats band, and a dark case-study panel with a carousel and arrows.
  - A marquee of award names.
  - A **dark "Let's talk" contact**: intro and email links left, form right, with radio **chips for "I am: Employer / Job seeker / Other"**.
- **Typography:** a light grotesk and small labels, restrained.
- **Colour roles:** white, near-black and one saturated blue, with blue only on bands, buttons and active states.
- **Spacing:** medium density with table-like rhythm.
- **Premium details:**
  1. A services grid built as one bordered table (shared lines, no separate cards).
  2. Chips as the first form question, which means less typing on a phone.
  3. A dark contact section that feels like a final, serious step.
- **Fits:** industrial (dark contact, table grid) and clean-local (the chip form).
- **Not for us:** the stats band and the awards marquee, which would be invented numbers and awards (Never Fabricate).

## R7: "Kensho" wellness hero (still)
`e83fd29526304c730df0dff7d8ebf8d7.webp`

- **For:** the hero.
- **Layout:**
  - A full-bleed soft photo.
  - A **huge light-weight headline** across the middle.
  - Directly under it, **a hairline row of four labels** in equal columns, continuing as faint vertical grid lines down the photo.
  - A subhead block bottom-left and a small product card bottom-right.
  - The nav has hairline dividers.
- **Typography:** a very large regular-weight grotesk (calm, not shouty) with small labels and body. The scale contrast is extreme and the weight contrast is minimal.
- **Colour roles:** warm greige, taken entirely from the photo, with off-white type. No accent at all.
- **Spacing:** very calm, with the grid giving the order.
- **Premium details:**
  1. The labels row under the headline turns the hero into a table of contents (for us, the services).
  2. The small card in the bottom-right corner (for us, the phone number).
  3. Grid lines that continue through the photo.
- **Fits:** heritage and clean-local (calm, warm). It hints at an "editorial" mood, but that's a variant, not a new preset. Heritage already owns warm and established.
